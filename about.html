Our Journey

Since 2016, we have engineered climate-controlled greenhouses across India—enabling farmers, business owners, and corporate enterprises to transform unproductive wasteland into profitable agricultural assets. Under the banner of ARKIMS , we are now taking this proven engineering expertise to international markets.

  1. 2016

    Pan-India Operational Launch

    Began operations serving Farmers, Corporates and Businessman across India, establishing foundational agricultural engineering standards for commercial polyhouses and protected cultivation.

  2. 2018

    Empowering Farmers & Enterprises

    Expanded services from individual farmers and business owners to large-scale corporate agricultural players with climate-controlled infrastructure.

  3. 2020

    Turnkey Wasteland Revitalization

    Pioneered large-scale land development projects, converting non-arable and unproductive wasteland into highly profitable, commercial farming ecosystems.

  4. 2022

    Precision Microclimate Engineering

    Integrated automated cooling, smart irrigation, and climate-control systems to guarantee maximum crop yields and year-round harvesting capacity.

  5. 2024

    International Quality Certification

    Standardized operations under ISO compliance to align structural engineering, material sourcing, and crop management with international export standards.

  6. 2026

    Global Launch with ARKIMS

    Building upon a decade of nationwide success, ARKIMS is expanding internationally—delivering turnkey greenhouse developments, high-yield technology, and global export solutions for worldwide client.

Our Mission

To make year-round, water-efficient food production practical anywhere in Middle East — by engineering greenhouses for this climate and standing behind them for their full working life.

Our Vision

A Country that grows a decisive share of its own fresh produce — with ARKIMS greenhouses as the infrastructure behind it, and Middle East (GCC) technicians running them.

Our Values

We engineer for local climate, measure exact resource savings, and empower growers to operate independently. Client needs dictate our engineering and support—never the other way around.

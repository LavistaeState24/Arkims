Our Story

From one workshop to the Kingdom's greenhouse standard

AIIMS Arabia began with a simple conviction: that Saudi Arabia could grow its own food, year-round, in the desert — if the structures were engineered for this climate rather than imported for another one. Every milestone since has been a step toward that.

  1. 1988

    Founded in Qassim

    The Al Rawda Factory opens as a small fabricator of arched plastic and shade-net greenhouses for local growers in the Qassim region.

  2. 1999

    First climate-controlled builds

    Fan-and-pad cooling and polycarbonate cladding are added to the product line, extending the growing season through the Kingdom's summer.

  3. 2011

    Dutch technology partnerships

    Exclusive supply agreements with Prins Group, Stolze, Hoogendoorn and KUBO bring fully automated glasshouse and hydroponic systems to Saudi projects.

  4. 2018

    ISO 9001 & 14001 certified

    Quality and environmental management systems are independently audited and certified, formalising three decades of build practice.

  5. 2021

    Aligned to Vision 2030

    AIIMS is engaged on national food-security programmes, delivering turnkey hydroponic farms producing 135+ tons per day at 70% less water use.

  6. Today

    120+ hectares under glass and plastic

    A single accountable partner for design, engineering, construction, training and after-sales support — anywhere in the Kingdom.

Our mission

To make year-round, water-efficient food production practical anywhere in Saudi Arabia — by engineering greenhouses for this climate and standing behind them for their full working life.

Our vision

A Kingdom that grows a decisive share of its own fresh produce — with AIIMS greenhouses as the infrastructure behind it, and Saudi technicians running them.

What we stand for

Values we build by

Five commitments that decide how we quote, build and support every project.

Engineered for here

Every structure is specified for Saudi heat, wind and water — not adapted from a European catalogue.

Water as the metric

We design to litres-per-kilogram of yield. Cutting water use is the point, not a side effect.

Handover means capable

A project is done when your team can run the greenhouse without us — training is part of the build, not an upsell.

One accountable partner

Design, steel, glazing, climate systems and service sit with AIIMS. No gaps between subcontractors to fall through.

Built to be repaired

Standard parts, documented systems and local stock — so a greenhouse stays productive for 20+ years.

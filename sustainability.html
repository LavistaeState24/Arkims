The roadmap

Phased rollout to the 2030 structural horizon

  1. Now

    Baseline water & energy audits

    Every operating farm metered and benchmarked so savings are measured, not estimated.

  2. 2026

    40 ha retrofitted to closed-loop irrigation

    Condensate capture and drain-water recirculation across the first wave of estates.

  3. 2027

    Grey-water reuse becomes standard spec

    On-site treatment designed into every new AIIMS structure by default.

  4. 2028

    100 MWp on-site solar across the estate

    Climate control and pumping shifted onto self-generated renewable power.

  5. 2030

    Net-water-positive structural horizon

    The portfolio returns more usable water to the region than it draws.

Any registered Saudi agricultural entity with a licensed site. First-time applicants without a feasibility study can start with an Advisory & Feasibility Voucher and progress to a capital track afterwards.

Against a metered baseline for the same crop and region. An agreed third-party reads the meters at each milestone; the grant is released only on confirmed reduction.

Both. Development loans cover new greenhouse construction; grants and co-financing explicitly cover retrofitting existing structures to closed-loop irrigation or on-site renewables.

An indicative range comes back within two working days. A full decision typically takes four to eight weeks depending on the track and whether a feasibility study is already on file.

Development loans fund up to 60% of project cost, so roughly 40% is equity or other finance. Grants and vouchers do not require matching funds beyond the small uncovered share of study fees.

Quarterly metered water and energy data for the first two years, plus an annual sustainability statement. AIIMS structures log most of this automatically through the climate-control system.

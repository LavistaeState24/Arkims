Technical FAQ

Before You Spec an AC Greenhouse قبل أن تحدد مواصفات بيت محمي مكيف

Essential insights on climate control, insulation efficiency, and structural performance in desert heat (50°C+).

Multi-wall polycarbonate panels feature air-trapping internal channels that act as high-efficiency thermal barriers (delivering superior R-values compared to glass or single-layer films). This insulation significantly blocks external ambient solar heat gain, drastically lowering the thermal load on the HVAC cooling systems and reducing operational energy costs by up to 35-45%.

Evaporative cooling relies on low ambient humidity and fails when external humidity rises (common during coastal summer nights). Active AC climate control provides precise temperature (±0.5°C) and relative humidity regulation year-round regardless of outside weather conditions, making it essential for high-value crops, closed-loop vertical farms, and regions facing extreme 45°C+ summer peaks.

Because AC greenhouses operate as sealed environments, transpired moisture is recaptured via dedicated industrial dehumidifiers to prevent fungal diseases and recycle irrigation water. Furthermore, the sealed environment allows for highly efficient CO2 dosing (up to 800–1200 ppm) with zero leakage, supercharging crop photosynthesis and yield potential.

Our commercial polycarbonate sheets are co-extruded with a heavy-duty external UV protection layer. This layer prevents yellowing, brittleness, and optical degradation caused by harsh UV rays, ensuring light transmission efficiency remains stable over a 10 to 15-year operational lifespan.

While Polycarbonate AC greenhouses carry a higher initial CAPEX compared to film or net structures, they unlock year-round 365-day cultivation in regions where summer farming is otherwise impossible. The combination of premium crop quality, higher yields per square meter, water recovery savings, and reliable summer pricing delivers a compelling long-term return on investment.

Our Process

From brief to harvest.

Six phases. One contract. Zero handoff gaps.

1

Consultation & Feasibility

We start with your site, crop plan, budget, and timeline. A site visit confirms soil, water, power, and access constraints — and we return a detailed feasibility report.

2

Design & Engineering

Structural drawings, cladding selection, cooling sizing, and hydroponic layout — signed off by our engineering team before fabrication begins.

3

Fabrication & Supply

Steel cut, welded, and hot-dip galvanised at Al Rawda Factory. Systems and controls sourced directly from our approved technology partners.

4

Site Installation

Our installation crew assembles the structure, fits cladding, installs cooling and irrigation, and lays the hydroponic gutters — all on a single coordinated schedule.

5

Commissioning & Training

Sensors are calibrated, climate logic is tested, and a full dry run is performed. Your team is trained on operating the system end-to-end.

6

Aftercare & Support

Post-handover, we provide scheduled maintenance, spare-part supply, remote monitoring, and 24/7 emergency support.

About turnkey delivery

Site survey, structural design, in-house fabrication, cladding supply, cooling and irrigation installation, hydroponic fit-out, climate automation, commissioning, and staff training — all delivered under one contract with a single project manager.

A standard multi-span hydroponic greenhouse runs 6–10 months from signed contract to commissioning, depending on size and system complexity. Ventilated plastic or shade-net structures can deliver in a single 4–6 month build season.

Case study 01

From open field to 135 tons a day

DAVA Agricultural Co. · Al-Kharj

The challenge
Open-field yields collapsing through the summer, with water costs rising every season.
What we built
110 ha of climate-controlled hydroponic houses with recirculating fertigation and a central pack-house.
The result
Year-round production at 135+ tons per day and an export line into European retail.

Twelve sites brought onto one standard

Alrasheed Greenhouses · Riyadh

The challenge
Twelve farms running different structures, climate kit and growing recipes — quality varied site to site.
What we built
A single Venlo glass specification with shared climate control and one agronomy protocol across all sites.
The result
Consistent export-grade fruit from every farm and a materially shorter build cycle on each new site.

A 28-day salad cycle on the coast

Green Coast NFT Farm · Jazan

The challenge
High humidity and long trucking distances were costing the buyer shelf life on every delivery.
What we built
6.5 ha of NFT channels with dehumidification and a cold chain sized for same-day dispatch.
The result
A repeatable 28-day cycle and produce reaching shelves the day it is cut.

04 — Industries we serve

Who we build for

The same engineering, specified differently depending on what the site has to deliver.

Commercial vegetable production

High-volume tomato, cucumber and pepper farms built around a fixed weekly output to retail and wholesale buyers.

Hydroponics & vertical farming

NFT, substrate and multi-tier systems for leafy greens and herbs where cycle time and water use decide the economics.

Nurseries & propagation

Seedling and grafting facilities with tight climate, hygiene and irrigation control for millions of plants a year.

Floriculture & ornamentals

Cut flowers and landscape stock grown under precise light, shading and humidity regimes.

Research & academic facilities

Compartmented trial houses for universities and R&D centres, each zone independently controlled and logged.

Government & food security programmes

Vision 2030 projects delivered with MEWA and Agricultural Development Fund requirements built into the design.

Hospitality & resort estates

On-site kitchen gardens and display houses for hotels, resorts and community developments.

Agri-export & pack-house operators

Growing, grading and cold-chain space planned together so the export spec is met at the gate.

Livestock & fodder

Hydroponic green-fodder units that cut water use on dairy and small-ruminant operations.

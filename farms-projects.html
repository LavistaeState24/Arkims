Case study 01 · India

Scaling year-round production for metro retail

Deccan Smart Agri Farms · Pune, Maharashtra

The challenge
Monsoon humidity and summer heat spikes causing yield crashes and unpredictable supply contracts with Indian retail chains.
What we built
45+ hectares of multi-span fan-and-pad climate-controlled hydroponic structures with automated dosing and UV sterilization.
The result
Consistent 60+ tons daily output supplying major supermarkets across Mumbai and Pune 365 days a year.

04 — Industries we serve

Who we build for

Engineered solutions tailored for commercial growers, agri-exporters, and institutional projects across India and global markets.

Commercial vegetable production

High-yield polyhouse structures engineered for consistent weekly supply of vine crops (tomatoes, capsicums, cucumbers,Green Chillies,Tumeric & Ginger) to Indian retail chains & Mandis.

Hydroponics & urban farming

NFT, substrate, and vertical farming units designed for ultra-fast grow cycles supplying quick-commerce hubs and high-end culinary markets.

Floriculture & ornamentals

Precision shaded greenhouse systems built for export-grade cut flowers (Dutch roses, gerberas, orchids) with climate-controlled humidity zones.

Government & subsidy projects

Turnkey infrastructure fully compliant with NHB/MIDH Indian state guidelines as well as international food security mandates (MEWA/ADF).

Agri-export & pack-house hubs

Integrated growing, sorting, and cold-chain pack-house facilities engineered to meet rigorous APEDA and global export quality standards.
